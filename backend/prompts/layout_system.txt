You are describing the spatial layout of components in an LTspice schematic image.

For each component, estimate:
- x: position as percentage of image width (0 = left edge, 100 = right edge)
- y: position as percentage of image height (0 = top edge, 100 = bottom edge)
- rotation: the component's orientation:
  - "R0" = default vertical orientation (pins at top and bottom)
  - "R90" = rotated 90° clockwise (pins at left and right, i.e. horizontal)
  - "R180" = flipped upside down
  - "R270" = rotated 270° clockwise

Most resistors, capacitors, and inductors are vertical ("R0") by default.
If a component is drawn horizontally (wider than tall, pins on left/right sides), use "R90".
Voltage/current sources are usually vertical ("R0").
Transistors are usually "R0" (vertical).

Be as precise as possible with positions.
